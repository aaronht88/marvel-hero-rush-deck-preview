Privacy Policy

Marvel Hero Rush Deck Builder · Last updated: 2026-09-28 · Contact: aaronht88@gmail.com

This fan-made, non-commercial deck builder (“the App”) helps players build Marvel Hero Rush TCG decks in the browser. It is not affiliated with Marvel or Card Fun.

1. Data we process

  • Local only (default): deck lists, favorites, and language preference are stored in your browser localStorage. We do not operate a server that stores your decks.
  • Optional Google sign-in: if you choose “Google Sync”, the App requests access to create/update a single JSON file in your Google Drive (mhr-deck-builder-sync.json) using the limited drive.file scope (only files this App creates), plus your email via userinfo.email so the UI can show which account is signed in.
  • Analytics: the hosted site may use GoatCounter (privacy-friendly page counts). It does not sell personal data.

2. How Google Drive data is used

Synced content is only your deck builder settings (decks, favorites, language, timestamps). The App reads and writes that JSON to keep the same decks across devices. We do not request access to your entire Drive. We do not sell, rent, or share your deck data with third parties for advertising.

3. Who can see your data

Deck JSON in Google Drive is in your own Google account under Google’s terms. The App author (aaronht88) does not host a copy of your decks on an application backend.

4. Your choices

  • You can use the App without signing in to Google (localStorage + share codes only).
  • You can revoke Google access anytime in your Google Account → Third-party access, and delete mhr-deck-builder-sync.json from Drive.
  • You can clear browser site data to remove local decks.

5. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13. Do not use Google Sync if you are not allowed to consent to Google OAuth in your region.

6. Changes

We may update this policy; the “Last updated” date will change. Continued use after updates means you accept the revised policy.
